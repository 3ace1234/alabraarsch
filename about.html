About Al-Falaah

Assalamu Alaikum. We are raising knowledgeable, confident, skilled and morally upright young people — prepared for both this world and the Hereafter.

Our Story

A modern school rooted in Islamic values

Al-Falaah was established with a clear purpose: to raise knowledgeable, confident, skilled and morally upright young people who are prepared for both this world and the Hereafter.

We believe education should go beyond simply preparing a child to pass examinations. A child needs sound academic knowledge, a strong Islamic foundation, practical digital skills, good communication, confidence, discipline and the ability to solve problems and work with others.

Our school combines Islamic education, conventional academic education, digital literacy and practical soft-skills development within one learning environment — from Nursery through Primary to Secondary.

A Word From The Proprietor

Why Al-Falaah was built

“When a parent visits our school, I want them to immediately understand and think — this is a school that is preparing my child for the future without compromising their Islamic values.”
  • Students understand the Qur'an and their religion deeply.
  • They perform well academically and understand the world.
  • They use technology responsibly and creatively.
  • They carry the personal skills needed to succeed in life.
Beyond a Typical School

How Al-Falaah is different

We are not simply an Islamic school that teaches the conventional curriculum — and not simply a conventional school with religion attached.

Grounded in Faith

Qur'an, character and daily practice are woven into school life, not an add-on subject.

Academically Strong

The full Nigerian curriculum with serious examination preparation at every level.

Digitally Ready

Real computer labs, coding and digital research — taught responsibly and productively.

Character-First

Communication, leadership, discipline and confidence developed as part of the timetable.

Our Vision

“To nurture knowledgeable, morally upright, digitally literate and confident young people who are prepared to positively contribute to their families, communities and society.”

Our Mission

“To provide quality academic and Islamic education while equipping students with digital literacy, practical soft skills and strong character for lifelong learning and responsible living.”
