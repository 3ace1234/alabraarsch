A complete learning model built on four pillars — Islamic education, academic excellence, digital literacy and soft skills — from Nursery through to SS 3.

The Educational Model

Four pillars, one complete education

Islamic Education

Faith, Qur'an, Islamic knowledge & character.

Digital Literacy

Technology, digital creativity & responsible use.

Soft Skills

Communication, leadership, teamwork & problem-solving.

Pillar 01 — Islamic Education

Faith, Qur'an and character

We want students to develop a strong understanding of Islam and good character — not simply to learn Islamic information, but to apply good Islamic values in their daily lives.

Qur'an Reading & MemorisationDaily recitation & Hifdh
TajweedCorrect, beautiful recitation
Hadith & SumahSayings of the Prophet (PBUH)
Aqeedah & FiqhBelief & everyday worship
Seerah & Islamic HistoryLife of the Prophet & civilisation
Adab & MannersCharacter in daily practice

Our Islamic aim

  • Memorise and understand the Qur'an.
  • Worship correctly and consistently.
  • Treat teachers, elders and peers with respect.
  • Live Islamic values both in and out of school.
  • Become ambassadors of good character.
