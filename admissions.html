Admission into Al-Falaah

We welcome children from Nursery through Secondary school. Begin your application online, or visit the school office to see us in person.

How to Apply

A simple, transparent process

1. Submit Application

Complete the online form below, or collect a paper form from the school office.

2. Assessment

An age-appropriate oral assessment or placement test for higher classes.

3. Interview

A friendly meeting with the prospective student and parent.

4. Enrolment

Acceptance, fee payment and confirmation of your child's space.

Levels We Admit

Every stage of growing up

From a child's first day in Nursery 1 to their final year in SS 3, Al-Falaah walks with them — academically, spiritually and practically.

  • Nursery: Nursery 1, 2 & 3 — ages 3–5.
  • Primary: Primary 1 – 6 — ages 6–11.
  • Junior Secondary: JSS 1 – 3 — ages 12–14.
  • Senior Secondary: SS 1 – 3 — ages 15–17.
Transfers from other schools are welcome mid-session.

For New Students

  • Child's birth certificate or age declaration.
  • Two passport photographs of the child.
  • Previous school report / transfer certificate (Primary 2 & up).
  • Parent / guardian identification.

Key Information

  • Admission period: opens each new session / term.
  • Terms: First, Second & Third terms per school year.
  • Schools hours: 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM (Mon – Fri).
  • Admission desk: call or WhatsApp us for the current guide.
