Al-Falaah Portal

One door for parents, teachers & the administration

A communication platform planned to bring results, attendance, timetables, announcements and school management together. The login below is the frontend interface — accounts will be enabled with the backend.

For Parents

  • Select your child and check results.
  • View attendance & the timetable.
  • Read announcements & upcoming events.

For Teachers

  • View assigned classes & timetable.
  • Record attendance & enter results.
  • Submit results for approval.

For Administration

  • Manage students, parents & teachers.
  • Manage classes, subjects & results.
  • Publish announcements & review admissions.

Roadmap — With The Backend

What the portal will do for you

The frontend you see now is the design. The next build connects it to real, secure accounts and data.

Results & Report Cards

Parents check their child's term results once published; teachers submit and the administration approves.

Attendance & Timetables

Live attendance records and class timetables available to parents, students and teachers.

Announcements & Events

The school publishes notices, events and important information to the right audience instantly.

Admission Management

Applications, assessments and enrolment tracked digitally from the office dashboard.

Reviews & Complaints
